An off-grid homestead rooted in sustainability, family, and long-view stewardship — solar powered, with every garden row and structure connected to living simply and intentionally. From thriving straw bale gardens and sweeping sunflower fields to hands-on demonstrations and free-roaming animals, you'll leave with a sense that this kind of life is possible, practical, and worth pursuing.

Tour Tips

Read each stop's highlights carefully — not all stops are open both days, and event hours and amenities vary.
Watch for Ag & Arts Tour signs marking important intersections and site locations.
Bring a cooler for perishable purchases — produce, meats, herb butters, flowers.
Bring cash! Many stops have limited Wi-Fi or no card machines.
Not every stop has a public bathroom — use one when and where it's available.
Parking is limited at many farms — please ride-share if you can.
Be kind, and leave our farms better than you found them.
The tour runs through the heart of rural Hickman County — stops are spread out, so take your time and enjoy the scenery as you go!
